<h2>Previous Work in Artificial Reverb</h2>

A selection of seminal articles on artificial reverberation, from Tom Erbe's lecture on the Erbe-Verb.

1. Schroeder, M. R. "*Natural Sounding Artificial Reverb*." Journal of the Audio Engineering Society, vol. 10, no. 3, pp. 219–223, 1962. [[AES E-Lib](https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=352)]

1. Gerzon, M. A. "*Synthetic Stereo Reverberation*." Studio Sound, parts 1–2, 1971. [[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Gerzon+Synthetic+Stereo+Reverberation+1971)] — no open-access DOI known; consult AES or Studio Sound archives.

1. Moorer, J. A. "*About This Reverberation Business*." Computer Music Journal, vol. 3, no. 2, pp. 13–28, 1979. [[DOI / JSTOR](https://doi.org/10.2307/3679680)]

1. Moore, C. "*Time-Modulated Delay System and Improved Reverberation Using Same*." U.S. Patent 4,170,721, 1979. [[Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4170721)]

1. Stautner, J. & Puckette, M. "*Designing Multichannel Reverberators*." Computer Music Journal, vol. 6, no. 1, pp. 52–65, 1982. [[DOI / JSTOR](https://doi.org/10.2307/3679704)]

1. Dattorro, J. "*Effect Design — Part 1: Reverberator and Other Filters*." Journal of the Audio Engineering Society, vol. 45, no. 9, pp. 660–684, 1997. [[AES E-Lib](https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=7524)] [[PDF (CCRMA)](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~dattorro/EffectDesignPart1.pdf)]

1. Jot, J.-M. "*Efficient Models for Reverberation and Distance Rendering in Computer Music and Virtual Audio Reality*." Proceedings of the International Computer Music Conference (ICMC), 1997. [[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Jot+Efficient+models+reverberation+distance+rendering+1997)] — no persistent open-access DOI; available via ICMC proceedings or IRCAM.

1. Rocchesso, D. "*Reverberation*." In DAFX: Digital Audio Effects, ed. U. Zölzer, Wiley, 2002. [[DOI (Wiley)](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298)] [[DAFX website](https://www.dafx.de/)]

1. Välimäki, V., Parker, J. D., Savioja, L., Smith, J. O. & Abel, J. S. "*Fifty Years of Artificial Reverberation*." IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing, vol. 20, no. 5, pp. 1421–1448, 2012. [[DOI (IEEE)](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASL.2012.2189567)]
